**Goal:** By 2026-11-10 (two days before the 2026-11-12 12:00 PT deadline) a public demo where a judge compiles a dive operator's real cancellation wording into cited rules, guests pay their deposit and trip balance through PayPal sandbox, a recorded official warning (replayed per D12) prompts the operator, whose cancellation settles inside the mandate as PayPal refunds or credit at the moment it is decided (D18) with a stop-and-ask on an unruled clause, and the Devpost submission points to this repo, the demo and a <3 min video. ## Global constraints and definition of done (stand-in; there is no master plan)

- Product contract in `AGENTS.md` is binding: model never supplies amounts; every rule cites a verified verbatim span; unruled clauses never auto-execute; settlement starts only from the operator's recorded cancellation, and an official CAP warning is a condition matched in code, never a trigger on its own (D16); Open-Meteo only corroborates; money moves only as captures the guest makes and as refunds or credit code computes, and a payment's refunds never exceed what it captured (D18); sandbox and synthetic guests only; attributed quotes and a neutral fictional demo operator.
- Provider keys and PayPal credentials stay server-side and out of git; nobody prints them; live model calls use synthetic data only.
- The dive-trip-agent fallback stack on the VM (compose project `dive-deposits-demo`, its nightly timer at 10:00 UTC, its secrets) is never modified by this plan.
- Every step after step 3 is accepted through `make check` plus the step's own commands; a step is ticked only after its acceptance ran in the orchestrator's environment; measurements and sandbox runs go to `docs/evidence.md`.
- Definition of done per product step: tests for the new behavior including one mutation that must fail; README or docs updated where behavior is user-visible; audit timeline covers new money or evidence events.

## Scope

- Do: requirements R1–R20 in `requirements.md`.
- Don't: currencies other than USD in v1 (amounts are USD minor units end to end); real operator onboarding or live PayPal (contract: sandbox only); guest accounts (link only); vouchers as PayPal objects (no native voucher); authorization holds, voids and reauthorization (D18); a fee setting of tidehold's own (deductions come only from the operator's wording, D18); dive-safety advice; Vault (not offered to the target merchant countries).
- Deferred: PayPal webhooks (trigger: D19 chooses an invoice with webhook notification, or a sandbox refund stays `PENDING` beyond the reconcile retries; step 10); returning PayPal's fee to the guest (trigger: an operator asks for it, or a target market's law requires it, D18 revocation triggers); CAP regions beyond D4 (trigger: step 1 shows them reachable and time left); paid model tier (trigger: judging starts, after the 2026-11-12 deadline; D6); tear-down of the dive-trip-agent stack (trigger: hackathon results announced).
